## mouse_cvh_hfd_gse104709

Mouse ventral hypothalamus - chronic high-fat diet — Mus musculus; bulk RNA-seq.

Chronic 45%-fat HFD versus regular chow in male C57BL/6J ventral/circumventricular hypothalamus. Five RNA pools per arm, each pooled from two mice; individual pool members are not deposited. Diet-switch arms are excluded. Technical sequencing runs were merged by the authors and are not replicates. The author supplied PORT-normalized resampled read counts, not raw counts. Same-symbol rows are summed on the deposited linear scale and transformed as log2(PORT-normalized read count + 1) for the matrix/limma route. All mice underwent three hours of forced wakefulness before ZT6-7 collection with food available. The chronic contrast includes diet and resulting obesity, and does not isolate diet from body weight. The downloaded file contains 21,654 Ensembl rows and 21,518 unique trimmed author symbols, whereas the paper reports 21,517 detected genes; the authentic file is retained without forcing its dimensions to match the paper.

Declaration: [src/data/hfdStudies.generated.json](<../../../src/data/hfdStudies.generated.json>).
