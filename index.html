Where a gene is expressed in the hypothalamus, and how it changes across public studies

Use HypoExplorer to examine hypothalamic differential expression and pathway enrichment in your browser. Explore genes and pathways within a dataset, or compare findings across studies. Each study retains its own experimental design and statistics.

Find a dataset or its source paper

The study directory lists GEO accession numbers, source citations, organisms and experimental comparisons for the data available in HypoExplorer. Every study page links to its original source and its interactive explorer.

Explore curated research topics, review the methods and limitations, and learn how to cite the original studies and HypoExplorer.

Topics include obesity, aging, feeding and fasting, circadian biology, and reproduction. Results come from reanalysis of public data. Each record credits the source study and its authors.
